About

My research looks at how to track characters across entire novels. Most coreference systems are built for short documents, so I build models and annotated corpora for book-length French fiction, then use the output for literary questions such as character gender and narrative structure.

Coreference resolution Computational literary studies French fiction Long-document NLP Narrative analysis

Software

Propp: Pattern Recognition and Ontologies for Prose Processing

A Natural Language Processing framework for narrative analysis. Includes the trained French coreference pipeline, available as the open-source propp_fr Python library.
